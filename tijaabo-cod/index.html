Dhageysi: Soomaali iyo Ingiriis (Eleven Labs v4)

Labada luqadoodba eleven_v4 ayaa ka hadlaa — Ingriisku hadda browser TTS robot ah ma aha. Riix luqadda, kadib mid dooro oo ii soo sheeg magaciisa.

Haddii aad doonayso inaad ka tagto bogga: riix “Door this voice” kaarka aad jeceshahay, kadib ii soo celi laptop-ka oo igu dhah “wad shaqada”. Bogga weyn ee wicitaanka: http://127.0.0.1:4173/call.html → Settings → Codadka internetka.
